About Pohoda

We build and sell our own apps.

Pohoda is a product studio in Tokyo. We design, build and operate our own consumer software: iOS apps on the App Store and web services. Every product is ours, and revenue comes from the people who use them. We do not do client work, contract development or consulting.

Business model

Freemium software, paid by users.

Our products are free to start. Users pay for premium features:

  • iOS apps: in-app subscriptions (monthly / yearly) and one-time purchases through the App Store.
  • AI features: paid plans and consumable AI credits (for example in Glidwell).
  • Web services: paid plans and reports sold online via Stripe (for example YANE Pro).

We are a technology business: we own the products, the code and the customer relationship. We do not sell services, hours or consulting.
