How each screen works
Home

Today at a glance.

Your latest weight and the change from last time, body fat, days since your last workout and the past two weeks of training, all on one home screen.

A summary of the month, with workouts, total volume and weight change, sits just below.

Log

Log a set, then move on.

Pick an exercise and your last time appears, with a gentle goal for today, such as “Last time 132.3lb × 11 reps. A little higher today.”

Choose a rest time between sets, from 30 to 150 seconds.

Exercises

Exercises, by body part.

Chest, back, shoulders and more. Each exercise shows what you did last time, and opens up to your history and estimated 1RM.

You decide which exercises to do. KATACHI remembers past sessions and suggests a gentle next target.

Trends

Your body’s changes, as a smooth line.

Look back at weight and body fat over a week, a month, three months, a year or all time, and set a goal weight to see where you stand.

Just the facts, with no pressure.

Plate calculator

Load the bar without the math.

Enter a target weight and choose your bar, and the plate calculator shows which plates to put on each side.

It sits alongside the rest timer and the other tools for your session.

Features

Log in seconds

Pick an exercise, enter weight and reps. Or type a line like “Bench Press 135lb 5 reps 3 sets” and it’s read for you. Running is logged too, by distance or by speed, time and incline.

Progress photos

Take front, side and back photos with a self-timer camera, with your last photo faintly overlaid so every shot lines up. Compare before and after, and export a timelapse. Faces can be pixelated automatically when you share.

Track your body

Weight and body fat trends, a goal weight, and optional import from Apple Health.

Monthly Log

A one-card recap of your month, with training days, volume and weight change, made to share or keep.

Tools

Plate calculator, rest timer with Live Activity, estimated 1RM and weekly volume by body part.

Units that fit

kg or lb, cm or ft/in, km or miles, ml or fl oz. Your choice.

Your body is yours.

Your records and photos stay on your device. There are no ads and no tracking, and an optional Face ID lock is available. KATACHI is a logging tool and does not provide medical advice. Read the Privacy Policy.

FAQ
Is KATACHI free?

Yes. KATACHI is free to use with the last 10 days visible. KATACHI Pro keeps your full history, with Apple Health import, iCloud backup and shareable Monthly Log cards. Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase (pay once, no renewal). Prices are shown on the App Store in the currency of your country or region.

Are my photos and records sent anywhere?

Your records and photos stay on your device. There are no ads and no tracking. Faces can be pixelated automatically when you share a photo.

Can I type a workout instead of tapping?

Yes. Type a line like “Bench Press 135lb 5 reps 3 sets” and it’s read for you.

Does it work with Apple Health?

Yes. KATACHI Pro offers optional import from Apple Health.

Which units can I use?

Does KATACHI give medical advice?

No. KATACHI is a logging tool and does not provide medical advice.
