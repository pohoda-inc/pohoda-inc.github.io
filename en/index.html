About Pohoda

Pohoda is a product studio in Tokyo that designs, builds and runs its own consumer apps. It was founded in May 2026 by Keisuke Sugimoto, as a sole proprietorship with the trade name Pohoda. “Pohoda” is a Czech word for a calm, comfortable ease.

The apps are free to start, with optional paid plans as in-app purchases. About Pohoda
