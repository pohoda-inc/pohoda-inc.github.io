How each screen works
Home

See who’s due for a hello.

Open the app and the people it’s time to reach out to are listed, along with upcoming birthdays. Each one has a Log button, so you can record a catch-up on the spot.

Set how often feels right for each person: weekly, monthly, or a few times a year.

Contacts

The people who matter, in one place.

Family, friends, partner and colleagues. Filter by category, and see when you were last in touch with each person.

It’s your circle, arranged your way.

Relationship map

Your connections, as a map.

You sit at the center. The people you connect with most are nearer the middle, and those you haven’t reached out to in a while drift outward. Direction shows the category.

A slow glow means it’s time to reach out.

Memories

Save the little moments.

Add a note or photo when you log a catch-up, and star the moments you want to keep. Memories gather person by person.

Look back on your year with the people you love.

Features

Relationship map

See your family, friends, partner and colleagues on a map. The people you connect with most sit near the center; those you haven’t reached out to in a while drift outward.

Gentle reminders

Get a quiet nudge when it’s time to reach out. Set how often feels right for each person, plus birthday and anniversary reminders.

Log in a tap

Tap “Talked today” to log a call, meeting, message or meal. Add a note or photo to remember the moment.

Home screen widget

See who to reach out to next, and upcoming birthdays, right on your home screen.

Memories and Year in Review

Star favorite moments, and look back on your year with the people you love. AI features run on your device with Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones.

Private by design

Your data stays on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking. Optional Face ID lock and backup to your own iCloud Drive.

FAQ
What does Keep In Touch do?

It helps you keep in touch with the people who matter. You set how often to reach out to each person, get a quiet reminder when it’s time, and remember birthdays and anniversaries.

Is it free?

Free for up to 5 people. The Basic plan removes the limit, with unlimited people and reminders. Choose a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time purchase (pay once, no renewal). Prices are shown on the App Store in the currency of your country or region.

What is the relationship map?

It shows your family, friends, partner and colleagues on a map with you at the center. The people you connect with most sit near the center, and those you haven’t reached out to in a while drift outward.

Is my data safe?

Your data stays on your device. There is no account, no ads and no tracking. You can add an optional Face ID lock, and back up to your own iCloud Drive.

Does it use AI?

Memories and Year in Review include AI features. They run on your device with Apple Intelligence on supported iPhones.
