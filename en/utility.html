Features

Four apps, one

Notes, a to-do list, your calendar and simple health tracking (weight, body fat %, steps), switched with the tabs along the bottom.

Notes you can find

Create, edit and delete notes freely. Pin the ones you need close at hand, and search to find the rest.

Tasks with due dates

Keep a to-do list with due dates and reminders. Tasks with a due date also show up in the calendar.

Your own Calendar

Synced with your device’s own Calendar: see your existing events alongside your notes and tasks, and add or edit events right from Utility. This is used only if you allow access.

Weight, body fat, steps and BMI

Log them by hand, or read them from the Health app (iPhone and iPad). BMI is calculated from your height, and charts show the trend over time. Choose kg/cm or lb/ft-in.

iPhone, iPad and Mac

Notes and tasks sync through your own iCloud, and you can back up to iCloud and restore any time. Choose Light, Dark or System appearance.

Privacy

Your records stay in your hands.

Utility makes no network calls except iCloud sync and the App Store. Notes and tasks are stored on your device and in your own iCloud. Health records never leave the device and are not stored in iCloud. Calendar and Health are used only if you allow access. There are no ads, no tracking and no account to register. FAQ
What is Utility?

Utility brings together notes, a to-do list, your calendar and simple health tracking (weight, body fat %, steps) in a single app, so the four apps you open every day become one. It works on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and has no ads.

Is it free?

Yes. You can save up to 10 notes, 10 tasks and 10 manual entries of each health metric (weight, body fat, steps) for free. The yearly Supporter plan (auto-renewing subscription) or a one-time purchase (pay once, no renewal) removes the limits and adds CSV export. Prices are shown on the App Store in the currency of your country or region.

Does it sync across my devices?

Notes and tasks sync through your own iCloud, and you can back them up to iCloud and restore any time. Health records (weight, body fat %, steps) never leave the device and are not stored in iCloud.

Does it work with Health and Calendar?

On iPhone and iPad, if you allow it, Utility can read steps, weight and body fat % from the Health app. On Mac, which has no Health app, you enter them by hand. Utility shows the events from your device’s own Calendar and lets you add or edit events from inside the app. Both are used only if you allow access.

Is my data sent anywhere?

Utility makes no network calls except iCloud sync and the App Store. There are no ads, no tracking and no account to register.
