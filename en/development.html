FAQ

How do we pay?

In full, in advance, by card through Stripe. Prices are in JPY.

Is there a contract?

Yes. We sign a short written agreement electronically before we start: what we build, the price, the schedule and who owns what.

Who owns the code?

Once the work is accepted, the copyright to the app, including the source code, is transferred to you.

Whose Apple Developer account is used?

Yours. The app is published under your company’s Apple Developer account, so it stays fully yours.

Do you use AI?

Yes. We only handle your information with tools set not to train on it.

A rough idea is enough. We reply within two business days.
